UX-001 · CHAPTER 01

“Done” is not always done.

A polished screen can announce success while the person is still unsure whether anything useful happened. To judge the experience, follow the entire attempt: the goal, the action, the service response, and what the person can do next.

User experience is what people perceive and how they respond as they use a product or service in pursuit of a goal. An interface contributes, but so do the information before it, the reliability behind it, and the consequences afterward.

What UX is

How to apply it

Take one ordinary product moment. Pull the thread.

Suppose an airline app says “Check-in complete,” but the boarding pass is missing. A screen review is not enough. The lesson asks you to follow the outcome.

  1. State the goal.The traveller needs a valid boarding pass, not merely a submitted form.
  2. Trace the response.What did the app say happened? What actually happened in the service?
  3. Look for recovery.Can the person find the pass, understand what remains, or get help?
  4. Make a decision.Is the fix clearer feedback, a better handoff, or a broken process behind the interface?
