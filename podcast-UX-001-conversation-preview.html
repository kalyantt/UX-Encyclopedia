A conversation, not a lecture

A proposed opening for “What UX is.” Alex and the guide discover the point through Maya’s situation, instead of taking turns explaining it.

Text preview only. This has not been recorded or added as an audio version. The complete Version 1 podcast remains the default. Read this to judge the direction of the conversation, not the voice.

Alex

Before we start… what do people actually mean by UX? Is it the screen?

Guide

That’s what we’re going to find out in this chapter. We’ll follow Maya as she tries to get an appointment, then see why a working screen can still leave her stuck.

Alex

That sounds uncomfortably familiar.

Guide

Look at her phone with me. It says, “Request received.”

Alex

Great. So she’s booked.

Guide

Does it say she’s booked?

Alex

…No. Just that they got the request.

Guide

And she’s already waited days for the referral. Imagine putting the phone down now.

Alex

I’d wonder whether to wait, call, or start over. I wouldn’t feel done.

Guide

Exactly. The screen gave feedback. Maya still doesn’t know if she’s closer to care.

Alex

So the experience isn’t just that message. It’s the whole attempt to get help.

Guide

Yes. What she needed, what she tried, what happened next, and what she could trust. That’s the question we’ll carry through the course.

Alex

I was ready to improve the confirmation screen. I hadn’t asked what it was confirming.
