THE CONTINUING CASE · 204 STEPS

One problem. The whole process.

CarePath is a fictional but realistic healthcare service. Its failures are not contained in one interface: they appear between search, booking, referrals, staff, payments, accessibility needs, policy, and follow-up. Alex will improve it one evidence-backed decision at a time.

Begin with step one →
THE PEOPLE

Patients and staff

Different abilities, languages, confidence, urgency, roles, devices, and access to support. No convenient “average user.”

THE PROBLEM

Fragmented progress

People repeat information, miss preparation steps, lose referrals, misunderstand cost, and cannot tell who owns recovery.

THE OUTCOME

Care that holds together

A service that helps people reach appropriate care safely, understandably, and with agency—without simply digitising every step.

The red thread through every lesson

Each stage produces an artifact or decision that the next stage inherits. The course never resets Alex to a blank page, and later lessons may challenge assumptions made earlier.
