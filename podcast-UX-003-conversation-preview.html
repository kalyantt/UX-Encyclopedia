Experience goals

A conversation-first opening for this lesson. Alex and the guide work through the idea together, continuing the CarePath story.

Text preview only. This has not been recorded or added as an audio version. The written lesson remains below the player in the course.

Guide

What did we learn from Maya's booking test?

Alex

A completed form isn't enough if she thinks a request is an appointment. We need the right outcome and the right understanding.

Guide

Good. Today we'll add another question: how should the experience leave her feeling, and what design choices would make that feeling justified?

Alex

The team already has words. Simple, friendly, seamless… delightful.

Guide

And Maya just saw confetti after sending a request. What does she do?

Alex

She calls reception. She still can't plan her day.

Guide

So is delight the right goal?

Alex

No. The celebration is almost insulting. She needs to know where she stands.

Guide

That's the difference between an experience goal and a mood board. Pick the quality the activity calls for, then make it change the design.

Alex

Let's try ‘confident.’

Guide

What would Maya need to see before that confidence was earned?

Alex

Her actual status, who acts next, when she'll hear back, and how to get help if nothing happens.

Guide

Good. What about control?

Alex

Let her review a consequential choice, save progress, and change the request without starting again. Don't make cancellation the only escape.

Guide

And respect?

Alex

Plain language. No blame for a missing document. Don't expose a sensitive clinic name in a notification. Offer a human route if the digital one fails.

Guide

Now each feeling has evidence. How would you know we'd missed?

Alex

If people call just to ask what's happening, abandon when they need to change something, or apologise because they don't understand our terms.

Guide

Exactly. Usability asks whether people can do the task effectively and safely. Experience goals ask what qualities the journey should have. They overlap, but one doesn't replace the other.

Alex

So if a bank transfer works, confetti might still be wrong. I would rather see the amount, recipient, timing, and a way to recover.

Guide

And we should test these goals in different conditions. That's where we're going next.
