Human-Centered Design Is an Evidence Practice

A conversation-first opening for this lesson. Alex and the guide work through the idea together, continuing the CarePath story.

This conversation has a short recorded preview in the lesson player. It is not the full episode. The written lesson remains in the course.

Alex

We have an experience brief, a usability scorecard, and a service map. Is that what makes CarePath human-centered?

Guide

Only if they can change a decision. In this lesson, we'll test the team's favorite explanation for why people abandon booking.

Alex

They say the form is too long. I can cut fields.

Guide

Maybe. What else could explain the drop-off?

Alex

People might not know which service they need. Their referral might be missing. The available times might not fit work. Or they may be afraid to submit the wrong information.

Guide

Good. Which evidence would tell those explanations apart?

Alex

The funnel shows where people leave, not why. I should watch real attempts and ask what people believed was happening.

Guide

And talk with staff. They see what happens after an incomplete referral enters the service.

Alex

When I watch Maya, she keeps moving between the form, her referral message, a calendar, and an insurance document. The fields aren't the only work.

Guide

What do staff see?

Alex

Calls, mismatched referrals, and manual re-entry. The form isn't just long; information is fragmented.

Guide

Could a shorter form still help?

Alex

Yes, but I shouldn't make it the whole intervention. I'd test clearer evidence requirements, referral status, saved progress, and a staff view that shares the same state.

Guide

Write down the decision, the assumption, the evidence, and what would change your mind. What's missing from your evidence so far?

Alex

People who cannot begin online at all. We need offline recruitment, or we'll call the service improved while leaving them out.

Guide

That's the practice: evidence and participation while choices are still changeable, then learning again after release.

Alex

So ‘human-centered’ isn't a workshop label. It's whether what we learn can reshape the work.
